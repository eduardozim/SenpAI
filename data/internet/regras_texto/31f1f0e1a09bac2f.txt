objects other than the core inside 
the sakigawa, and the chigiri at 
the end of the tsuka.

that significantly impair safety 
must not be used.

The required thickness shall be  
 
determined by the smallest 
diameter of the tip of the 
sakigawa ( distance between 
opposite sides), and the diameter 
of the chikuto (distance between 
opposing corners 8cm from the 
tip of the shinai). The tip of the 
shinai shall be the narrowest  
part of the chikuto, which shall 
become broader from the tip to 
the monouchi. 
Article 3: Kendo-gu referred to 
in Article 4 of the “Regulations” 
is illustrated in Figure 3. 
1. The men-buton shall be long 
enough to protect the shoulder 
joints and have sufficient shock-
absorbing capacity to cushion 
the impact of a strike. 
2. Kote shall protect at least one-
half of the forearm (the longest 
section between the elbow and  
the wrist). The kote fist and the 
kote-buton shall have sufficient 
shock-absorbing capacity. 
3. The gouge in the  kote-buton  
shall not exceed 2.5cm between 
its longest and shortest parts.

not meet the criteria of Article 3

given by the shinpan-in after the 
match.
